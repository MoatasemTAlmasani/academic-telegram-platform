==================================================

1. DATABASE TECHNOLOGY
   ==================================================

Use:

- PostgreSQL
- SQLAlchemy 2.x
- Alembic
- Pydantic v2 where schemas are required

Use SQLAlchemy declarative models with clear relationships.

Use UUIDs or another robust identifier strategy for primary keys where appropriate.

Do not use sequential public IDs for sensitive resources.

==================================================
2. DATABASE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Follow:

- normalization
- referential integrity
- foreign keys
- appropriate indexes
- unique constraints
- NOT NULL constraints where appropriate
- timestamps
- soft deletion where appropriate
- auditability
- scalability

Avoid premature over-engineering.

Do not duplicate data unnecessarily.
